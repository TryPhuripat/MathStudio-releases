# Math Studio for Windows

An equation editor for **Word, PowerPoint, Excel and Visio**. Build equations from palettes or type LaTeX, see them as you go, and put them in your document as sharp vector pictures that you can open and edit again later.

By TryPhuripat.

## Install

1. Open **[Releases](https://github.com/TryPhuripat/MathStudio-releases/releases/latest)** and download `MathStudioSetup-<version>.exe`.
2. Close Word, PowerPoint, Excel and Visio.
3. Run the setup program. You don't need administrator rights.
   - Windows may show **"Windows protected your PC"** because the program isn't signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
4. Open Word. The **Math Studio** tab appears on the ribbon.

You need Windows 10 or 11 and desktop Office (Microsoft 365, or Office 2016 or later).

## Licence

The first time you open Math Studio, it asks for a licence:

- **CMU students and staff:** click **Sign In** and sign in with your `@cmu.ac.th` account.
- **Everyone else:** paste the product key you were given and click **Activate**.

You can see your licence any time under **Math Studio → License** on the ribbon.

## Updates

Math Studio updates itself. It looks for a new version once a day when Office starts. You can also check with **Math Studio → Check for Updates**.

## Uninstall

Go to **Settings → Apps → Installed apps**, find **Math Studio**, and choose **Uninstall**.

## Problems or ideas

Please tell TryPhuripat. A screenshot and the version number (from **Math Studio → About**) help a lot.
